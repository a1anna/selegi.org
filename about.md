SELEGI is a community-driven digital asset built on the Stellar network and inspired by the people, culture, and connections of the Pacific.

Our goal is simple: explore easier ways for families, vendors, and communities — whether you're back home, across the oceans, or somewhere in between — to send, spend, get paid, and stay connected.

Because sending value across oceans shouldn't feel like sending a message in a bottle — or waiting on a carrier pigeon. 🌊🐦

We're starting small, learning as we go, and building with the Pacific in mind.

SELEGI is currently an early-stage experimental project, so the journey is just beginning.

**No promises of Lambos. Just an idea, a blockchain, and a whole lot of ocean between us.**
